    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Four stages, built from the official descriptions. PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 in difficulty and about 200 hours, with one playthrough enough — there are no online trophies, no difficulty settings and no mission replay, so save manually at the start and end of each chapter. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off.

    Missable trophies

    Built from the official descriptions and the published guides. Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable. Everything else — story trophies included — can be finished after the story in free roam.

    Why nothing else is missable

    • Story trophies (chapters and named missions) pop as you play the story and cannot be missed.
    • Stranger strands stay open after the story — every quest giver tied to a trophy comes back in free roam.
    • Collectibles in story buildings that later close also spawn in other locations.
    • Quests with an hourglass expire, but they do not count toward any trophy.
    • 100% Completion has nothing missable on its checklist; progress made during the story already counts.

    Sources: PowerPyx GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap; GTABase; the official trophy list on Exophase.

    About & sources

    Leonida Platinum is a free, open-source, offline guide. It runs entirely from its own files — no network requests, no account, no Rockstar logo or official artwork. Your checklist lives in your browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back.

    Where the facts come from

    • Names, descriptions and grades — the official PlayStation 5 list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles (18 Nov 2026), the two agreeing on all 55: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden.
    • Counts (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone race locations, 18 meditation spots, 100 WAiNK locations, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garage properties, 500 choices) — PSN's progress targets as listed by PSNProfiles.
    • Methods and roadmap — PowerPyx's GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap and GTABase, paraphrased in this guide's own words and cited beside each trophy.
    • Honesty rule — where no published method exists yet (five trophies as of 20 Nov 2026), the guide says so plainly instead of inventing a location or step.

    How to use it

    • Tick a trophy when you earn it — it stays ticked when you reload.
    • Turn on Hide story spoilers to blank out the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them.
    • Use the Roadmap for the order to play in, and the Missables page for the one thing to do before Chapter 1 ends.
    • Export your progress before clearing browser data; import it back any time.
