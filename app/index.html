    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours (PowerPyx's estimate; difficulty 4/10). The order below is built from the official trophy descriptions: story first, the 100% checklist after the credits, then cleanup. Nothing except the Chapter 1 meditation can be missed, and the checklist only opens after the story — so there is no wrong way to play the story itself.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable by the guides that have played the game. Everything else can be earned after the story in free-roam.

    How not to lock yourself out

    • Keep cheats off. Cheats disable trophies and saving (PowerPyx, GTABase).
    • Save by hand. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — the game gives you 15 manual save slots plus one autosave (PowerPyx).
    • Do the Chapter 1 meditation. It is the one thing in this guide that can go away for good; see the card above.
    • Don't sweat the rest. Quest givers who vanish for a while (Kenton, for example) come back later, and optional missions that expire are never the ones a trophy needs — there are far more than the 100 the checklist asks for (PowerPyx).

    About this guide

    Leonida Ledger is a free, open-source, offline trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5. It runs entirely from its own files — no network requests, no account, no ads. Your progress lives in this browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back.

    The list

    55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden on PlayStation until earned. Names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles on 18 November 2026. There are no online trophies — every trophy is single-player.

    How the guides were written

    Each guide is written in our own words for this guide, from the official description plus the launch-era guides that had played the game. Every guide names its sources beside it, so you can check any fact. Where no source had confirmed a trigger yet — one trophy, “Bad Luck, I Guess” — the guide says so plainly instead of guessing. A cited guide beats an uncited one, however detailed; a guessed step helps nobody.
